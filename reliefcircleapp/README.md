## project idea
Project Abstract: Community-Driven Relief Fund
Project Name: ReliefCircle
The Community-Driven Relief Fund is a transparent and decentralized fundraising platform that allows charities to request funds, donors to contribute, and volunteers to verify fund utilization. The platform provides real-time tracking of donations and ensures funds are used effectively by allowing donors to participate in the verification process.
This system enhances trust and accountability by enabling charities to submit proof of fund usage, which is reviewed by volunteer donors before marking the charity as verified. The platform leverages cloud-based storage, secure payment processing, and automated tracking to create a seamless and trustworthy donation experience.
Users & Their Roles:
Fundraisers (Charities)
Submit fund requests with detailed descriptions.
Track donations in real-time.
Upload proof (images/documents) of fund utilization.
Donors
Donate funds to charities via an easy-to-use dashboard.
Track past and ongoing donations.
Optionally sign up as volunteers to verify fund utilization.
Volunteers (Donor-Verifiers)
Review uploaded proof documents and images.
Approve or flag fund utilization.
Once a charity is sufficiently verified, it receives a "Verified" badge on the platform.
